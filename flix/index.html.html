How Compare Bay, Wear Vault, and Recipe Bench are meant to be used

Compare Bay lets you store a dual-slot record: a swatch plate photograph beside an on-nail photograph, with optional marks for color shift, thickness, gloss, and cat-eye difference, plus titles, notes, style tags, season tags, and occasion tags. You may search and filter those records, open a read-only detail view, edit a record, or delete it from the local vault.

Wear Vault lets you store a wear session: a cover image, days worn, storage notes, removal notes, optional fail-review flags and fail reasons, and the same family of tags. You may filter for fail reviews, open a timeline-style detail view, edit, or delete.

Recipe Bench lets you store layered gel recipes (base, color, effect, and top with cure seconds), colorway presets with brand codes, and bottle inventory with quantity and expiry. You may open an insight view of a recipe, edit or delete recipes, presets, and bottles, and use shelf-alert preferences you set on the workspace desk.

Nail Studio is a drawing surface on bundled plate photographs. You may paint with gel, glitter, or charm tools, adjust stroke width, undo or clear, and export a design image back into a dual-slot record. Exported designs are treated as material you created in the App.

Use these tools for your own documentation of nail DIY work. Do not use Flix to harass others, to store material you have no right to use, or to present a personal log as a public laboratory result or a medical claim.
